# Slide Add-on: Business Model & Revenue Pathway

## Monetization (Post-Hackathon)
- B2B SaaS licensing for city EMS / hospital networks
- Usage-based orchestration/API pricing for routing decisions
- Enterprise integration + support contracts

## Payment Methods (Phase-appropriate)
- Pilot: Invoice / PO (institutional buyers)
- Expansion: ACH + card for smaller providers
- Platform: metered billing for API usage

## Governance & Trust
- HIPAA-aware data handling roadmap
- Audit logs and explainable decision traces
- KYC/compliance only when handling regulated payment flows

## Why this is credible
- Starts with procurement-friendly B2B model
- Avoids consumer-payment complexity in MVP
- Scales with measurable operational savings
